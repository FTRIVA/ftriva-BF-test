YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

How information submitted through the FTRIVA website is intended to be handled.

Who is responsible for your information?

FTRIVA is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact ftrivafitness@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on the service you choose, FTRIVA may collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, parent or guardian contact details where applicable, package or membership selection, payment reference and transaction details, and information you provide about your goals, training history, activity, equipment, schedule, injuries, pain, medical conditions, medication and exercise restrictions.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, administer Founding 10 claims, process and verify purchases, manage memberships, assess exercise readiness, prepare and manage online fitness programmes, communicate with clients, maintain records and protect the service against fraud or misuse.

Health and injury information

Information about injuries, pain, medical conditions, medication and exercise restrictions can be sensitive. Please provide only information relevant to safe exercise programming. It is used for programme suitability and safety review, not for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you report concerning symptoms or restrictions, FTRIVA may ask you to obtain guidance or clearance from an appropriate healthcare professional before exercise programming proceeds.

Children and guardian consent

If a client is under 18, the assessment asks for parent or guardian contact details and confirmation that guardian consent has been obtained. A parent or guardian should complete or supervise the process and contact FTRIVA with any questions about a minor's information.

Payments

Paystack processes checkout and payment credentials. FTRIVA does not receive or store full card numbers or card security codes. The website stores transaction references and relevant payment confirmation information so that purchases and memberships can be recorded and reconciled.

Service providers and where information is processed

The website and its server-side functions are hosted through Cloudflare. FTRIVA uses Supabase to store customer, purchase, membership and assessment records, and Paystack to process payments. Generic sign-up notification emails may be sent through Cloudflare Email Service to FTRIVA. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

The Supabase database used for this website is hosted in the EU Central region (Frankfurt, Germany). This means some personal information may be stored outside South Africa. FTRIVA must ensure that any cross-border processing is supported by the safeguards and conditions required by applicable South African data-protection law before accepting client information.

How long we keep information

FTRIVA keeps personal information only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purposes described above, to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain appropriate business records or meet legal obligations. Retention and deletion procedures should be applied to the database and connected service providers.

Security

FTRIVA uses server-side access to the database and restricts public access to customer and assessment records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not send payment card details or unrelated sensitive information by email or messaging.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct personal information, ask questions about how it is used, or request deletion where FTRIVA is not required or otherwise permitted to retain it. You may also raise a concern about how your information has been handled. Contact ftrivafitness@gmail.com.

Updates to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The version published on this page applies from the date it is posted.

Important: This is a working privacy notice, not legal advice. Before launch, FTRIVA should have the notice and its cross-border data arrangements reviewed for compliance with POPIA and any other applicable requirements.
