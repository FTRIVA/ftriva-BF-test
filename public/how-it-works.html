How FTRIVA works.

A clear journey from choosing your package to receiving a programme built around your information.

01

Choose

Explore the packages or complete the short recommendation questionnaire.

02

Checkout

Enter your details and pay securely through Paystack.

03

Assess

After payment verification, complete your detailed fitness assessment and request a start date.

04

Prepare

Your information is reviewed and your programme is prepared before it becomes available.

Programme start dates: Your requested start date is recorded automatically. Programme access begins on that date only after the programme has been published and any required safety review is complete.
Start your journey

Good to know.

Is FTRIVA online only?

Yes. FTRIVA provides online fitness programming and support. No in-person training sessions or consultations are offered.

When do I complete the detailed assessment?

After your payment has been successfully verified.

When does my programme begin?

Your requested start date is recorded during assessment. Access depends on the programme being published and any required safety review being complete.
