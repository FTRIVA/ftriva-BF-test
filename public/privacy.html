YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

How information submitted through the FTRIVA website is intended to be handled.

Information we collect

When you enquire or purchase a programme, FTRIVA may collect contact details, payment references, training goals, training history, schedule preferences and information relevant to exercise safety.

Why we use it

Information is used to process purchases, verify payment, prepare and manage online training programmes, communicate with clients and support service delivery.

Health and safety information

Please provide accurate information relevant to exercise participation. FTRIVA provides fitness programming, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Information indicating a need for further review may require follow-up before programming.

Payment information

Card and payment credentials are handled by Paystack. FTRIVA does not store full card details on this website.

Security and contact

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be applied to personal information. For privacy questions or requests, contact ftrivafitness@gmail.com.

This notice is a starting privacy disclosure and should be reviewed against the final deployed data flows and applicable South African privacy requirements before accepting clients.
